ABOUT THE FIRM

Experience that

Pennington Business Solutions was founded on a straightforward belief: organizations work better when their processes are clear, their tools are useful, and their people have room to focus on what matters.

Led by Jason Pennington, Founder & Principal Consultant, the firm draws on more than 20 years of experience in business operations, including 12 years in human resources. That background brings a practical understanding of real-world workflows, organizational challenges, and the importance of thoughtful, responsible solutions.

We work with businesses that want to improve how things get done—whether that means refining an existing process, creating better visibility into information, or developing a tool that removes unnecessary work.
